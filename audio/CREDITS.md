# Sound credits

All sounds are real field recordings from the **radio aporee ::: maps** archive, hosted by the Internet Archive.
For this wallpaper each one was **trimmed to a clean section, high-pass filtered to remove wind rumble and traffic hum,
low-pass filtered at 15.5 kHz, loudness-normalised (−23 LUFS) and re-encoded as MP3**.
Files derived from CC BY-SA recordings are shared under the same licence (CC BY-SA 3.0).
